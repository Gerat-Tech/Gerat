# Gerat Services Page Content - Version 2

## Page goal

The Services page should answer one question quickly:

**What can Gerat actually help my business build or improve?**

Keep the structure easy to scan. Four service groups are enough. Do not turn every capability into a separate service category.

## Services page rules

- Keep exactly four top-level service groups.
- Do not create 10-15 small services that make the company look unfocused.
- Lead with business purpose, then list technology capabilities.
- Avoid technology-name dumping. Mention stacks only on technical/project pages where relevant.
- Do not promise outcomes that depend on unknown data or implementation conditions.
