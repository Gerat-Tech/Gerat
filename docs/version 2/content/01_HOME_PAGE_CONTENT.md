# Gerat Home Page Content - Version 2

## Page goal

The homepage is not a catalog. It is Gerat's first conversation with a potential client.

It should tell one short story while the visitor scrolls:

**Businesses face friction -> Gerat helps turn that friction into useful digital systems -> here is what we build -> here is how we work -> here are the five founders -> start a conversation.**

Keep the page visually rich but text-light. Use animation to reveal the story, not to make the visitor read more.

---

## 01. HERO - THE PROMISE

### Eyebrow
**GERAT SOFTWARE SOLUTION**

### Headline
**BUILD WHAT MOVES YOUR BUSINESS FORWARD.**

### Supporting copy
**We build websites, AI solutions, business systems, and brand identities that help businesses work better and grow with confidence.**

### Primary CTA
**START A PROJECT**

### Secondary CTA
**EXPLORE WHAT WE BUILD**

### Scroll cue
**SCROLL TO EXPLORE**

### Design/interaction direction
The hero should feel calm and confident. Use the brand bridge idea visually. Avoid a large block of explanatory text. The first scroll should naturally pull the visitor into the problem/idea section.

## 02. THE PROBLEM - WHY GERAT EXISTS

### Section label
**THE GAP**

### Headline
**GOOD BUSINESSES NEED GOOD SYSTEMS.**

### Copy
**A business can lose time in disconnected tools, unclear processes, weak digital experiences, or technology that cannot grow with it. Gerat exists to close that gap.**

### Closing line
**We turn business needs into technology people can actually use.**

### Design/interaction direction
Make this a strong transition section. Use progressive text reveal or a change in the visual composition. The section should feel like the visitor has moved from the brand statement into Gerat's reason for existing.

---

## 04. HOW WE WORK

### Section label
**THE PROCESS**

### Headline
**START WITH THE PROBLEM. BUILD THE RIGHT THING.**

### Intro
**We keep the process clear: understand the business, shape the solution, build it well, then stay close after launch.**

### Step 01
**UNDERSTAND**
We learn how the business works, where the friction is, and what the technology needs to achieve.

### Step 02
**DEFINE**
We turn the problem into a clear scope, user experience, technical direction, and delivery plan.

### Step 03
**BUILD**
We design and engineer the product, system, or brand with the right level of technology for the job.

### Step 04
**SUPPORT**
We help with launch, handover, improvements, and the next stage of the system.

### Design/interaction direction
Make this a scroll-driven sequence. Keep the current step visually dominant while the others remain quiet. The visitor should feel progress from 01 to 04.

---

## 05. THE GERAT POINT OF VIEW

### Section label
**WHAT WE BELIEVE**

### Headline
**USEFUL OVER COMPLICATED.**

### Principle 01
**USEFUL**
Technology should solve a real problem before it tries to impress.

### Principle 02
**CLEAR**
Good systems are easier to understand, use, and improve.

### Principle 03
**STRONG**
Good foundations matter because businesses have to live with what we build.

### Principle 04
**GROWING**
We build with the next stage in mind, not only today's requirement.

### Design/interaction direction
This section should be short and editorial. Use large typography with subtle scroll reveal. Do not turn these into four long paragraphs.

## 06. FIVE FOUNDERS - THE PEOPLE BEHIND GERAT

### Section label
**THE FOUNDERS**

### Headline
**FIVE FOUNDERS. ONE DIRECTION.**

### Copy
**Gerat was started by five founders who bring different strengths to one shared idea: build useful technology, build it well, and build a company that can grow.**

### Team cards
Use the five current founder names and approved titles from the source code. Keep homepage cards compact:

- Name
- Role
- One-line specialty only, if needed

Do not place full biographies on the homepage.

### CTA
**MEET THE FOUNDERS**

### Design/interaction direction
Use a clean 5-person composition that feels intentional rather than cramped. The five founder concept is part of the brand identity, so the visual system can subtly echo the five-part logo structure.

---

## Homepage content rules

- Do not add testimonials, client logos, statistics, awards, or case studies unless real and approved.
- Do not repeat the full services list in the footer and another homepage section if the navigation already exposes it.
- Do not use the old "Selected Work" section until there are genuine projects.
- Do not use the old long methodology copy such as "mission-critical platforms" or numerical claims unless verified.
- Keep the full homepage copy comfortably scannable in a few minutes.
- Every section must answer a new question.
