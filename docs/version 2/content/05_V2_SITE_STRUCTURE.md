# Gerat Website V2 - Page Structure and Content Map

## Primary navigation

**Home | Services | About | Contact**

Primary CTA: **START A PROJECT**

## What changed

### Portfolio -> About
Gerat is starting out. The website should not behave like a mature agency with a large case-study catalog. Replace the current Portfolio position with an About page focused on the founding story, brand idea, principles, five founders, and future direction.

### Insights -> Remove for now
Do not keep an empty or low-volume Insights section only because it exists in the template. Reintroduce it when Gerat has real original writing, research, announcements, or technical notes worth publishing consistently.

### Team -> Merge into About
The five founders should appear on Home as a compact trust section and on About with more detail. A separate Team page is optional later.

### Work / Case Studies -> Future
Create a Work/Case Studies section only when there are genuine projects with permission to show the work and specific outcomes that can be verified.

## Page responsibilities

### Home
**Purpose:** Explain Gerat quickly and create a story through scroll.

**Questions answered:**
- Who is Gerat?
- Why does Gerat exist?
- What does Gerat build?
- How does Gerat work?
- Who is behind it?
- What should I do next?

### Services
**Purpose:** Explain the four service groups and how they fit together.

**Questions answered:**
- Can Gerat solve my type of problem?
- Which service category fits?
- How does an engagement work?

### About
**Purpose:** Make the company memorable and credible as a young company.

**Questions answered:**
- Why was Gerat started?
- What does the name mean?
- What does the company believe?
- Who are the founders?
- Where is the company going?

### Contact
**Purpose:** Turn interest into a conversation with minimal friction.

## Content hierarchy rule

Do not move the same explanation from one page to another just to fill space.

Home = introduction.
Services = capability details.
About = company story and people.
Contact = action.

## Future expansion

When Gerat has enough real material, add:

1. Work / Case Studies
2. Individual Case Study pages
3. Insights / Journal
4. Dedicated Team page, if needed

Do not add these sections until they have meaningful content.
