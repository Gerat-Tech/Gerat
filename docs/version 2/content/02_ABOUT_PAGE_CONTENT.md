# Gerat About Page Content - Version 2

## Page goal

The About page should make a new company feel real, intentional, and memorable.

Do not try to make Gerat look old or oversized. The strength of the page should come from a clear founding story, the five founders, the company's point of view, and a believable direction for the future.

Recommended narrative:

**Who we are -> why we started -> what Gerat means -> what we believe -> who founded it -> where we are going -> start a conversation.**

---

## 01. HERO

### Eyebrow
**ABOUT GERAT**

### Headline
**A NEW COMPANY. A CLEAR DIRECTION.**

### Supporting copy
**Gerat is a software and IT solutions company building useful digital products, intelligent tools, business systems, and brand identities for growing businesses.**

### CTA
**MEET THE FOUNDERS**

---

## 02. OUR STORY

### Headline
**WE STARTED WITH A SIMPLE IDEA.**

### Copy
**Businesses do not need more technology for the sake of technology. They need better tools for the work they already do, clearer digital experiences, and systems that can grow with them. Gerat was started to bring those pieces together.**

### Closing line
**We are starting small, building carefully, and creating the foundation for a company that can grow with the businesses it serves.**

### Agent note
This is intentionally present-tense and honest for a 2026 company. Do not add invented founding events or a dramatic backstory.

---

## 03. WHAT GERAT MEANS

### Headline
**A NAME BUILT AROUND USEFULNESS.**

### Copy
**Gerat takes its name inspiration from the German word _Gerät_ - a tool, device, or piece of equipment made for a purpose. That idea fits how we think about software: it should be useful, purposeful, and built to do a job well.**

### Small brand line
**TECHNOLOGY IS A TOOL. MAKE IT USEFUL.**

### Agent note
Keep the umlaut in the explanatory German word "Gerät". Keep the company name "Gerat" exactly as branded.

---

## 04. THE GERAT IDEA

### Headline
**WE BUILD THE BRIDGE.**

### Copy
**Our brand is built around the idea of a bridge: connecting a business to better digital experiences, better systems, and better ways of working.**

### Four short principles
**SUPPORT** - Technology should help people do their work better.

**CONNECTION** - Good systems connect people, information, and processes.

**SCALABILITY** - What works today should leave room for tomorrow.

**OWNERSHIP** - We care about what happens after delivery, not only the launch.

### Agent note
These ideas are directly aligned with the Gerat brand presentation: support, bridge, scalability, and the founder group.

---

## 05. WHAT WE BELIEVE

### Headline
**SIMPLE PRINCIPLES. HIGH STANDARDS.**

### Principle 01
**SOLVE THE REAL PROBLEM.**
Start with the business need, not the technology trend.

### Principle 02
**MAKE COMPLEX THINGS CLEAR.**
Good software should reduce friction, not create more of it.

### Principle 03
**BUILD WITH THE NEXT STEP IN MIND.**
Design systems that can evolve as the business grows.

### Principle 04
**STAY ACCOUNTABLE.**
The relationship does not end when the product goes live.

---

## 06. THE FOUNDERS

### Headline
**FIVE FOUNDERS. ONE VISION.**

### Copy
**Five founders came together with different strengths and one direction: build useful technology and build Gerat for the long term.**

### Team presentation
Use the five approved founder profiles. On the About page, each profile can include:

- Name
- Role
- 1-2 sentence biography
- Core responsibility
- Optional professional links

Do not use long corporate biographies. Every founder should be understandable in one quick scan.

### Optional founder introduction line
**Different strengths. Shared ownership. One company we are building together.**

---

## 07. WHERE WE ARE GOING

### Headline
**STARTING NOW. BUILDING FOR WHAT COMES NEXT.**

### Copy
**Gerat is at the beginning of its journey. Our focus now is simple: earn trust through the work, build strong systems, learn from every project, and grow into a technology company known for reliability, clarity, and useful innovation.**

### Direction points
**BUILD** - Deliver real products and systems that solve real business needs.

**LEARN** - Improve through every engagement and every iteration.

**GROW** - Expand our capabilities without losing the quality that earned trust.

### Important
Do not convert this into a fake milestone timeline. A future roadmap may be added later when Gerat has real milestones to report.

---

## 08. FINAL CTA

### Headline
**LET'S BUILD WHAT'S NEXT.**

### Copy
**Have a business problem, digital idea, or system that needs work? Start the conversation.**

### CTA
**START A PROJECT**

---

## About page writing rules

- Make the company feel human, not corporate.
- Put the founding story near the top.
- Use the five founders as a genuine differentiator, not as decoration.
- Use the German name idea once; do not over-explain it.
- Do not claim years of experience as a company beyond its 2026 founding.
- Do not claim completed client work, awards, partnerships, or customer scale unless verified.
- Keep the page visually spacious. Strong headlines should carry much of the story.
