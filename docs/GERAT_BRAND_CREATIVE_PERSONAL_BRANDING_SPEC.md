# 2. CORE PRINCIPLE: LOGO ≠ BRAND

A logo is one visual mark. A brand identity is the broader system around it.

A complete brand system can include:

```text
Brand strategy
Positioning
Messaging direction
Logo system
Colour system
Typography
Graphic language
Iconography
Image direction
Motion direction
Templates
Digital applications
Print applications
Brand guidelines
```

Adobe's current guidance describes a brand guide as a blueprint covering typography, logo variations, image rules, color palettes, illustrations/icons and potentially voice/messaging. citeturn370944search0

### Website implication

Do not market only:

> Logo Design

Market:

> **Logo & Brand Identity**

Then offer a smaller logo-only engagement for clients who truly need only a logo.
